Pulse — Partner Playbook

What to do before there's anything to sell

Four tracks of useful work. All executable right now, without needing access to the product.

0 of 15 tasks complete
Track 1 — Build the prospect list
When we're ready to sell, this list is everything. Building it now means day-one momentum.
Map your existing network for salon/spa owners
Who do you already know? Warm intros are 10x easier than cold outreach. Start there.
Research 20–30 target businesses in your area
Google Maps, Yelp, Instagram. Find owner-operated salons with 2–10 staff. Note their tools where visible.
Find the owner's name and contact for each
Instagram bio, Google business listing, LinkedIn. You're selling to a person, not a business.
Build it in a simple spreadsheet
Name, business, contact, notes. Four columns is enough. The habit of capturing it matters more than the tool.
Track 2 — Do discovery conversations
Talking to owners before you pitch them is the single most useful thing you can do right now.
Have 5 casual conversations with salon/spa owners
Not a pitch. Genuine curiosity. "How do you keep track of your business performance?" "What's your biggest headache?"
Ask about their tools and what frustrates them
What do they use? Do they look at the analytics? What question do they wish they could answer easily?
Write up what you hear after each conversation
Even just a paragraph. Patterns across 5 conversations will shape how we pitch and what language we use.
Share notes back with the founder
This is product research as much as sales groundwork. The feedback loop matters.
Track 3 — Develop the sales pitch
Build it now so it's ready the moment there's something to show.
Write a one-paragraph pitch in your own words
How would you explain Pulse to a salon owner over coffee? Write it like you'd say it — not like a brochure.
Anticipate the top 5 objections and draft responses
"I already have Square analytics." "I don't have time for another tool." Work these out now, not on a live call.
Draft a short cold outreach message
DM, email, or text. Keep it 3–4 sentences. The goal is a conversation, not a sale.
Role-play the pitch with the founder at least once
Have them play a skeptical salon owner. Awkward moments in practice won't happen on real calls.
Track 4 — Know the landscape
Prospects will mention competitors. Know what's out there and how Pulse is different.
Spend 30 minutes with Square, Vagaro, and 7shifts
Look at their analytics sections. What do they show? Where do they fall short?
Look up Thryv and Broadly briefly
These are the "replace everything" platforms. Pulse is different — we connect, we don't replace.
Write one sentence for each: "They do X, we do Y"
Simple, honest, not aggressive. Your handle whenever a prospect asks how Pulse is different.
The goal: by the time there's something to demo, you walk in with a warm list, a tested pitch, real insight into what owners care about, and a clear answer for every objection. That's not prep work — that's a head start most salespeople never have.
