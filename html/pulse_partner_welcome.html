How your progress saves
Your checkmarks save automatically in the browser you're using. You don't need to create an account or do anything special. Just check things off and close the tab — it remembers.

One thing to know: progress saves per browser, per device. If you check things off on your phone, those checkmarks won't appear on your laptop — and vice versa. Pick one device as your main one and stick to it for the checklist.
If your progress disappears: this can happen if you clear your browser history or cookies. If it does, just re-check what you've done — takes about 30 seconds. The checklist itself never changes unless we update it.
